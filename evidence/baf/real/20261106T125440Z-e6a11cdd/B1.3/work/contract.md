# Service agreement

The supplier provides a monthly operational report. The service fee is 48,000 rubles per month.
Reports must arrive within 8 business days after month end. The customer has 6 business days to accept a report or describe defects.
Valid invoices are payable within 21 calendar days of receipt. The initial term is 12 months.
Each party keeps confidential information secure for 24 months after termination.
The supplier corrects accepted defects within 5 business days at no additional charge.
The parties first attempt to settle disputes through written negotiations. A court with jurisdiction resolves disputes that remain open.
All prices include applicable taxes. Amendments require signatures from both parties. No attachments or other pricing schedules form part of this agreement.
